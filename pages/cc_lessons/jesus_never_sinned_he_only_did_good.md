# Jesus never sinned; He only did good    
## 1 Peter 2:21-23 

### *"Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that ye should follow his steps: who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth"*

Children need to know that sin involves doing something wrong or not following God’s rules. 
Repentance is about feeling sorry for what they’ve done and asking for God for forgiveness.

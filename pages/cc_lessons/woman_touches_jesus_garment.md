# A woman touches Jesus and is healed
### Matthew 9:20-22; Mark 5:25-34; Luke 8:43-48

There were many people all around Jesus. Everyone wanted to be close to Jesus. Everyone wanted to hear what He said. Everyone wanted to see what He did. Suddenly, Jesus asked, "Who touched me?"

Jesus' friends looked around. "There are many people here. We're all bumping into each other," they said.

"No," Jesus replied. "Someone touched me. I know that power has gone out from me."

A woman came and bowed before Jesus. "I touched you because I wanted to be healed. I have been sick for many years. As soon as I touched you, I could tell that I wasn't sick anymore!"

Jesus smiled and said, "Your faith has healed you. Go in peace."

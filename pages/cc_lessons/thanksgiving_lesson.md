## 1 Thessalonians 5:18  

### *"In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you"*

## Thankful game
Have the children sit in a circle. Start by holding the ball and saying, “I am thankful for…” and finish the sentence with something you’re grateful for. Pass the ball to the next child, who does the same.

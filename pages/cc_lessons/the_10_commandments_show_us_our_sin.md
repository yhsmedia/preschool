### Romans 3:23

#### *"All have sinned, and come short of the glory of God"*

We have all sinned!. God is happy when we do the right thing. When we disobey God's rules, he is unhappy. The Bible says that we all disobey sometimes. We are all sinners. 
Have you ever lied? Have you ever disobeyed your mom or dad? Sure you have, you're a sinner.

**Note to teacher:**

Please use the visuals. This is a proven way for children to remember. Lets make it a goal that every child can recite the ten commandments. Not necessarily the verse, but the meaning of the verse, i.e., "God is # 1", or "No Idols". 

Please review all the previous commandments and their visuals. 

In teaching the ten commandments, we are laying the foundation for the children to understand salvation. Children need to know that sin involves doing something wrong or not following God’s rules. I like to tell them that sin is when we do something that gives Jesus a frowny face. ☹️

Repentance is about feeling sorry for what they’ve done and asking God for forgiveness, turning away from bad choices.

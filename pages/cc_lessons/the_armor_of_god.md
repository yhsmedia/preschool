# The Armor of God
## Ephesians 6:11-18

* Belt of Truth - Don't listen to Satan's lies
* Breastplate of Righteousness - Do right
* Shoes of the Gospel - Tell others about Jesus
* Shield of Faith - Trust God to help you
* Helmet of Salvation - Protect your mind
* Sword of the Spirit - Know the Bible

# No Stealing
## Exodus 20:15
### *“Thou shalt not steal”*

**Note to teacher:**

Please use the visual. This is a proven way for children to remember. Lets make it a goal that every child can recite the ten commandments. Not necessarily the verse, but the meaning of the verse, i.e., "God is # 1", or "No Idols". 

Please review all the previous commandments and their visuals. 

In teaching the ten commandments, we are laying the foundation for the children to understand salvation. Children need to know that sin involves doing something wrong or not following God’s rules. I like to tell them that sin is when we do something that gives Jesus a frowny face. ☹️

Repentance is about feeling sorry for what they’ve done and asking God for forgiveness, turning away from bad choices.

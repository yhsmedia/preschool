# The Bible is God’s Word
## 2 Timothy 3:15-17

* We need to obey the Bible.
* The Bible tells us to obey.
* The Bible tells us to be thankful.
* The Bible tells us to be kind.
* The Bible tells us to go to church.
* The Bible tells us to listen.
* The Bible tells us to pray.
* The Bible tells us to follow Jesus.
>  The Bible is true, through and through. There are no mistakes in it!
